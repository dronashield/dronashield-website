Course Overview

What CEH v13 Covers, and Why It Matters

The Certified Ethical Hacker (CEH) credential from EC-Council is one of the most widely recognised benchmarks in offensive security, and CEH v13 is the newest revision of that blueprint, the first version to formally weave AI-assisted reconnaissance and automation into its twenty-module curriculum. At DronaShield, a cyber security training institute in Baner, Pune, our CEH v13 training program follows this updated blueprint closely, but we do not stop at slides. Every module is reinforced with a hands-on lab inside our own hacking range, so you leave understanding not just what a vulnerability is, but how to find it, exploit it responsibly, and document it the way a real SOC or red team would expect.

Whether you are a fresh engineering graduate exploring a career in cyber security, an IT administrator who wants to understand how attackers view your network, or a working professional planning a switch into ethical hacking, this CEH v13 training in Pune is designed to take you from foundational networking concepts through to advanced exploitation, web application attacks, wireless security and cloud attack surfaces. Classes run out of our Baner campus in a hybrid format, so students from Balewadi, Wakad, Aundh, Hinjewadi, Pashan and across Pune can attend evening or weekend batches without disrupting a day job or an ongoing degree.

Every batch works inside an isolated lab network stocked with intentionally vulnerable machines, real CVEs and MITRE ATT&CK-mapped attack scenarios, using the same tools working analysts rely on daily: Nmap, Metasploit, Burp Suite, Wireshark, Nessus, Hydra and more. Instead of memorising tool syntax, you practice the full attack lifecycle of reconnaissance, scanning and enumeration, gaining access, maintaining access and covering tracks, followed by the defensive side of the same story, so you understand how a SOC analyst would detect and respond to what you just did.

On completion, students receive a DronaShield Institute certificate documenting the modules covered and labs completed, and are guided through exam preparation for the official EC-Council CEH v13 certification exam, one of the most widely requested certifications in Indian and global cyber security job listings. Our placement cell works alongside the training team to connect job-ready students with hiring partners across SOC analyst, VAPT and penetration testing roles across Pune and beyond.

Before You Start

Prerequisites

CEH v13 is written to work both as a first certification and as a step up from CompTIA Security+ or similar foundational credentials. You do not need prior hacking experience to join, but the following will help you get the most out of the program.

  • Basic familiarity with how computers, operating systems and the internet work.
  • Comfort using a command line is helpful but not mandatory as the first modules cover Linux and networking fundamentals from the ground up.
  • A laptop capable of running a virtual machine, with 8GB RAM or more recommended for the lab environment.
  • No prior certification is required to enroll in this CEH v13 training in Pune.

Live Labs & Projects

Theory alone does not prepare you for a real assessment, so every CEH v13 batch spends the majority of class time inside our isolated lab range. You will fingerprint and enumerate deliberately vulnerable machines, chain together real CVEs mapped to MITRE ATT&CK techniques, break into misconfigured web applications, capture and crack wireless handshakes, and finish with a capstone project where you scope, execute and report on a simulated engagement end-to-end, exactly like a junior VAPT consultant would on the job.
