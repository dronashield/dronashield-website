Course Overview

What This OSINT Course Covers

Open Source Intelligence, or OSINT, is the skill of finding, verifying and connecting publicly available information to build a clear picture of a person, organisation or threat. DronaShield's OSINT course in Baner, Pune teaches this as a structured investigative discipline, useful across cyber security, threat intelligence, due diligence and corporate investigation roles.

The course covers advanced search techniques and search engine operators, social media investigation and SOCMINT, people search and identity verification, geolocation from images and videos, domain and network reconnaissance, dark web and breach data monitoring, and automation using tools like Maltego and SpiderFoot. Ethical and legal boundaries are emphasised throughout.

Batches run in a hybrid format from our Baner campus, with students from Balewadi, Wakad, Hinjewadi, Aundh and Pashan attending evening or weekend sessions around existing jobs or studies. Every module includes hands-on practice investigating realistic scenarios, and the course closes with a capstone project running an end-to-end OSINT investigation and writing up findings professionally.

Graduates leave with a DronaShield Institute completion certificate, practical experience across leading OSINT tools, and research skills valued in cyber security, threat intelligence and investigative roles. Our placement cell works with job-ready students on resume reviews, mock interviews and introductions to hiring partners across Pune's growing threat intelligence industry.

Before You Start

Prerequisites

OSINT is one of the more accessible cyber security specialisations, so prerequisites are minimal.

  • Basic computer literacy and comfort using web browsers and search engines.
  • Curiosity, patience and attention to detail for research-heavy work.
  • A laptop with a stable internet connection for lab exercises.
  • No formal certification is required to enroll in this OSINT course in Pune.
